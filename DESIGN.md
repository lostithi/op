# Design

## Overview

A paper poetry archive, taken from the Variant mock the client pinned: bone field, black hairlines, three-column masthead and fact grid. Libre Baskerville for the masthead; Inter for the record. Malayalam titles stay in Noto Serif Malayalam. Invented prizes, London, emails, and poems from that mock are not used.

## Colors

Bone `#E7E9E4` and black. No laterite rooms.

## Typography

Libre Baskerville, uppercase, ~38px, line-height 0.9 for POETRY / SURESH / CHEEKODE / KERALA. Center mark `op`. Inter 11px for metadata, works, awards. Featured Tajmahal in italic Baskerville.

## Layout

40px page pad. Header is 3 equal columns. Meta grid 3 columns. Works and awards are label + two entry columns. Footer is a huge `op` mark and a right-aligned colophon.

## Elevation & Depth

Hairlines only. Entry hover is a 3% black wash and a crosshair cursor.

## Shapes

Square. Ornament is an 8px black square.

## Components

Masthead is the header. Nav is 11px under `op`. Contact is copy, not a letter card.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do

- Keep the archive grammar: masthead, fact grid, hairline, list, colophon.
- Mark Wikipedia-only awards with an asterisk.

### Don't

- Restore teletext, Knowledge Panel chrome, or monsoon laterite rooms.
- Invent a likeness, mailbox, London base, or poem.
